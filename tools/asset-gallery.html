How your verdicts work

Each card shows my provisional decision, why I made it, the exact artwork ID and the original PNG. You can vote Keep, Revise, or Reject independently. Your choices stay in your browser; export the JSON to share them for actual approval and integration. There is no hidden auto-accept, no deletion of rejected artwork, and no silent overwrite of the originals.

Artwork is shown on a true transparency checkerboard. Candidates that should contain transparency but do not are labeled Blocked. Some backgrounds are too painterly for the sprite style and remain alternative references rather than secretly deployed scenery.
