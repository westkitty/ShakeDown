    HOW TO PLAY — quick reference

    Manual: hold a direction to run and lift your finger to stop. Flick up to jump or down to dash. Turn on Auto-Run only when you want Cinnamon to keep running. Jump, Swish, Dash, and Cheer remain separate right-thumb actions.

    Gamepad: left stick/D-pad moves, A jumps, B dashes, X swishes, Y cheers, Start pauses. Press F for fullscreen on a keyboard. On iPhone Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App to remove the Safari address bar.
